What Porchlight does

Porchlight shows the packages you're expecting and what their carriers report about them. It is for tracking your own deliveries: packages you're sending, receiving, or otherwise have a reason to follow.

Your account

You sign in with your email address or your Apple account. Keep access to them secure, because anyone who can sign in as you can see your packages. You can delete your account at any time from Settings.

Carrier information

Statuses, delivery dates and scan history come from the carriers and from a tracking service. Porchlight passes them on as it receives them and never makes up a date, but carrier data can be late, incomplete or wrong. Don't rely on it for anything where a missed or late delivery would cause real harm.

Amazon and Flint

The Mac and iPhone apps can read your own Amazon orders after you sign in to Amazon on that device. This works on a best-effort basis: Amazon can change its pages or ask you to sign in again at any time, and imports stop until you do. Your use of your Amazon account is also covered by Amazon's own terms. The same goes for Flint and your email provider.

Fair use

Don't use Porchlight to follow other people's packages without their permission, to overload the service or the carriers behind it, or to get into anyone else's account.

Your data
