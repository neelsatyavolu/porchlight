Most packages add themselves.

A package gets into Porchlight one of three ways. After a one-time setup, the first two need nothing from you.

The big carriers, and the small ones.

Most carriers update on their own: the server checks every 15 minutes once a package is out for delivery and every hour or two while it's in transit. USPS, UPS and FedEx turn away servers, so the Mac and iPhone apps read their tracking pages while Porchlight is open. Each package says where its status came from and when.

  • USPSWhen the Mac or iPhone app is open
  • UPSOn its own, or from the Mac or iPhone app
  • FedExWhen the Mac or iPhone app is open
  • DHL ExpressThrough DHL's own tracking API
  • DHL eCommerceOn its own
  • Amazon LogisticsOn its own
  • OnTrac and LaserShipOn its own
  • UniUni and JitsuOn its own

Anything else goes through Ship24, which covers over a thousand more carriers. Porchlight's Ship24 allowance is small, so it's saved for numbers nothing above can read.

What leaves your devices

Porchlight needs your tracking numbers. It doesn't need your Amazon password or your email.

Stays on your device

  • Your Amazon password and sign-in cookies. They're kept on the Mac or iPhone where you signed in, and never synced.
  • The text of your email. Flint reads it on your Mac.
  • Camera images from label scans.

Sent to Porchlight

  • From Flint: the tracking number, carrier, sender and subject line.
  • From Amazon: order and shipment numbers, item names, the delivery date and the tracking number.
  • To the carrier or Ship24: the tracking number, and nothing about you.

No ads, and nothing is sold. This site counts page views without cookies, and the apps ask before collecting any analytics. Read the privacy policy
