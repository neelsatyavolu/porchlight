What Porchlight sees from your devices

Porchlight needs your tracking numbers. It doesn't need your Amazon password or your email.

Kept on your Mac or iPhone

  • Your Amazon password and sign-in cookies
  • The text of your email
  • Camera images from label scans

Sent to the Porchlight server

  • From Flint: number, carrier, sender, subject
  • From Amazon: orders, items, dates, numbers
  • To carriers: the number, nothing about you

The carriers Porchlight reads

The server checks every 15 minutes once a package is out for delivery. USPS, UPS and FedEx turn away servers, so the Mac and iPhone apps read those pages while open. Ship24 covers over a thousand more.

One list on your Mac, your iPhone and the web
